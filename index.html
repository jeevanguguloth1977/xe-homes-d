Our Story

Started at a kitchen table, still run that way.

Nutrek began with one simple frustration: most "premium" dry fruits on the shelf were old stock dressed up in nice packaging. We started sourcing directly from growers, sorting every batch by hand, and selling only what we'd keep in our own pantry. That's still the only standard we hold ourselves to.
